Tidemark

Pour to the line. Sixty seconds.

Tidemark is a one-screen arcade game for iPhone. A cup slides under the tap; hold anywhere to pour and let go so the drink settles exactly on the dashed line. The stream keeps falling after you let go, the tap speeds up, and every glass fills a little differently.

How it plays: hold to pour, let go to stop. Close to the line is Good, dead on is Perfect, past the rim is a spill. Every 5 in a row raises your multiplier up to ×5, and the tap pours faster while your streak lasts. The last 10 seconds score double.

After every round: a score card image to share, your best and average, and optional Game Center leaderboards, all-time and weekly.
