Tidemark

One cup. One line. Don't spill.

Tidemark is a pouring game for iPhone, played on one screen. A cup glides under the tap; press and hold anywhere to pour, then release so the drink comes to rest on the dashed line. The stream keeps falling after you let go, the tap quickens, and every glass fills a little differently.

How it plays: hold to pour, let go to stop. Close to the line is Good, dead on is Perfect, past the rim is a spill. Every 5 in a row raises your multiplier up to ×5, and the tap pours faster while your streak lasts. The final 10 seconds are the last call: every point counts double.

When the cup is done: share a receipt of your round and every cup you poured, see your best and average, and compare on the optional Game Center boards, all-time or weekly.
