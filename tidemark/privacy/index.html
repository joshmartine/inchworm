Tidemark

Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Tidemark is a pouring game that runs without a connection, except when Game Center is involved. Contact: admin@joshmartine.com.

What is saved here

  • Your statistics: best score, best streak, rounds poured, total points and the last 20 round scores.
  • A record of how your best round built up, second by second, used for the pace readout in later rounds.
  • Switches for sound, haptics, music and reduced motion.

All of it sits in the app's preferences on your iPhone. Reset Stats in Settings clears the statistics and pace record, and removing the app clears the rest.

Game Center

When you are signed in to Game Center, each finished round's score goes to the Tidemark all-time and weekly leaderboards. Apple displays it with your Game Center name; Apple's policy governs that (apple.com/legal/privacy). Tidemark itself uses your Game Center name only to show you are signed in, and holds an unsent score on the device until it can be delivered. If you are signed out, nothing is sent.

Score images

After a round you can share a picture of your receipt. The picture is made on the device and passed to the iOS share sheet, so it goes only where you choose.

Left out on purpose

Tidemark has no account of its own, no analytics, no ads, no in-app purchases and no outside code. It asks for no permissions, and apart from Game Center it never uses the network. You pay once through the App Store, and Apple handles that payment. We see anonymous crash data only when you have chosen to share it with developers in iOS Settings.

Children, updates

No personal information is collected from any player, including children. Changes to this policy will be posted here with a new date.
