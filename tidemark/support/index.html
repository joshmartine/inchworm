How do I play?

Touch and hold anywhere on the screen to pour into the cup, and let go to stop. Try to make the drink settle exactly on the dashed line. The shaded band around the line is the Good zone; dead on the line is Perfect.

Why does it keep filling after I let go?

The stream that has already left the tap still lands in the cup. Let go just before the surface reaches the line. The faster the tap pours, the earlier you need to let go.

How does scoring work?

A Perfect pour is worth 30 points and a Good pour 10, times your multiplier. Every 5 in a row raises the multiplier by one, up to ×5, and the tap pours a little faster at each step. A pour that stops short, overshoots the Good zone or spills resets it to ×1. Brim cups (gold rim) score double, and in the last 10 seconds every point counts double.

What are the different glasses?

Mugs and tumblers fill evenly. Wide-topped glasses fill slowly near the top; narrow flasks fill fast near the top. Two-tone cups have two lines: pour the first drink to the lower line, then the second drink to the upper line.

Is every round the same?

No. The cups and lines are different every round, but the speed-up and the last call follow the same schedule, so your score reflects your play.

How do I share my score?

At the end of a round, tap the share button under the score card. It shares an image with your score, best, combo, accuracy and a strip of every pour.

Where are the leaderboards?

Sign in to Game Center in the iPhone Settings app. A trophy button then appears on the title and results screens, with all-time and weekly leaderboards. Without Game Center the game works fully and your best score is kept on your iPhone.

Can I turn off sound, vibration or motion?

Yes, in Settings (the gear on the title screen). Sound also follows your iPhone's silent switch. Reduce Motion turns off screen shake and big splashes, and is always on when Reduce Motion is on in iOS.

Can I reset my stats?

Yes. Settings → Your Stats → Reset Stats. This only clears the scores kept on your iPhone, not your Game Center scores.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Only the Game Center leaderboards need a connection.
