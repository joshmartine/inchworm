Binpop

Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

Binpop: Quick Sorting Game ("Binpop", "the app") does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

What the app stores

Your best score, recent scores, stats and settings are saved only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the app sends your score at the end of each round to Apple's Game Center leaderboards, where other players can see it with your Game Center name. Game Center is run by Apple and Apple's privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy. You can play without Game Center; your scores then stay on your iPhone.

No accounts, tracking or ads

Binpop has no sign-in of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking code. Apart from Game Center it makes no network requests, and it does not use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Purchase

Binpop is a paid app bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. There are no in-app purchases.

Crash reports

If you choose to share crash reports and usage data with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports. These do not identify you.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: see the Support page.
