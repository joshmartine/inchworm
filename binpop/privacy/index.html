Binpop

Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Binpop is an offline arcade game with one optional online feature, Game Center. Contact: admin@joshmartine.com.

Scores on your iPhone

Binpop saves your best score, best combo, number of rounds, total points and your last 20 scores. It also saves the second-by-second score curve of your best round so the game can show your pace against it, plus your sound, haptics, music and reduce-motion choices. Settings has a Reset Stats button that erases these. Deleting the app erases them too.

Game Center leaderboards

If you are signed in to Game Center in iOS Settings, Binpop sends each round's final score to two Game Center leaderboards, all-time and weekly. Apple shows the score with your Game Center name to other players and applies its own privacy terms (apple.com/legal/privacy). The game reads your Game Center display name only to show that you are signed in. If a score cannot be sent yet, it waits on the phone until it can. Not signed in? Nothing is sent and the game plays the same.

Sharing a result

The share button on the results screen builds an image of your score on the device and gives it to the iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes.

What Binpop does not touch

There is no Binpop account, no analytics, no advertising, no in-app purchase and no code from other companies. The game requests no permissions: not photos, camera, microphone, location or contacts. Apart from Game Center it makes no network requests. The game is bought once in the App Store and Apple processes the payment. Anonymous crash reports may reach us only if you allow developer sharing in iOS Settings.

Children, changes

We collect no data about any player. New versions of this policy will be dated at the top.
