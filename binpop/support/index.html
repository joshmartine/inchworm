Support

Tips and answers. If something isn't covered, email admin@joshmartine.com.

How do I play?

Swipe left or right on a block to send it into the bin with the same color and shape. Tap gray junk to smash it; junk can't go in a bin. Gold stars fit either bin and are worth 50 points. Anything that reaches the end of the belt breaks your combo.

How does scoring work?

A sorted block or smashed junk is worth 10 points times your multiplier. Every 8 in a row raises the multiplier by one, up to ×5. A wrong bin or a dropped block resets it to ×1. The final 10 seconds are the Rush: every point counts double.

Why did the bins change sides?

The bins swap a few times in every round. "SWAP!" appears and the bins blink just before it happens. Check the bins before you swipe.

Is every round the same?

No. The order of blocks is different every round, but the speed-up, swaps and final rush follow the same schedule, so your score reflects your play.

How do I share my score?

When the round ends, tap the share button under the score ticket. The image shows your score, rank, best, combo, accuracy and a timeline of your multiplier through the round, and the message carries the App Store link.

Where are the leaderboards?

Sign in to Game Center in the iPhone Settings app and a trophy button shows up on the title and results screens, opening the all-time and weekly boards. Signed out, Binpop still plays normally and keeps your best on your iPhone.

Can I turn off sound, vibration or motion?

Yes. Open Settings from the gear on the title screen. Sound is also muted by the silent switch, and the music stays quiet while another app is playing audio. Reduce Motion drops the screen shake and big bursts, and it stays on whenever iOS Reduce Motion is on.

Can I reset my stats?

Yes. Settings → Your Stats → Reset Stats. This only clears the scores kept on your iPhone, not your Game Center scores.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Only the Game Center boards need a connection; the belt itself runs without one.

Getting a refund

Binpop is sold and billed by Apple. Ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
