Binpop

Sort fast. Sixty seconds.

Binpop is a one-screen arcade game for iPhone. Blocks ride down a belt; swipe each one into the bin with the same color and shape before it falls off the end. The belt speeds up, junk needs smashing, and the bins swap sides when you least expect it.

How it plays: swipe left or right to sort, tap gray junk to smash it, and swipe gold stars into either bin. Every 8 in a row raises your multiplier up to ×5. The last 10 seconds score double.

After every round: a score card image to share, your best and average, and optional Game Center leaderboards, all-time and weekly.
