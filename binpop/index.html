Binpop

Match it. Swipe it. Pop it.

Binpop is a one-screen sorting game for iPhone. Blocks ride a belt toward two bins: flick each one into the bin that matches its color and shape before it drops off the end. The belt keeps speeding up, gray junk needs smashing, and the bins swap sides when you least expect it.

How it plays: swipe left or right to sort, tap gray junk to smash it, and swipe gold stars into either bin. Every 8 in a row raises your multiplier up to ×5. The final 10 seconds are the Rush: every point counts double.

Between rounds: share a ticket with your score, rank and a second-by-second combo timeline, check your best and average, and climb the optional Game Center boards, all-time or weekly.
