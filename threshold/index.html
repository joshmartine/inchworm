Threshold

Qualify buyers, price homes and show the math.

Threshold is a real estate and mortgage calculator for loan officers, real estate agents, investors and home buyers. Every answer shows how it was calculated, with the formula and your numbers, and the math is tested against published worked examples.

Free to use: monthly payment with taxes, insurance, PMI and HOA (PITI); loan amount and loan-to-value; amortization summary; saved scenarios and PDF sharing.

Threshold Pro, one purchase with no monthly fee: qualifying with front and back-end debt-to-income ratios (conventional, FHA, VA or your own), maximum loan and income needed, affordability, the full amortization schedule with extra payments and bi-weekly, refinance break-even, rent vs buy, closing costs and cash to close, APR from fees, seller net sheet, investment property returns, commissions, dates and prorations.

Results are estimates, not a loan approval or lending decision.
