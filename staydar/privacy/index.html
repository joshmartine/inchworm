Staydar

Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

Staydar: Tax Residency Tracker ("Staydar", "the app") does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

What the app stores

Your trips (countries, dates and notes) and your rules are saved only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data. When you export a CSV file or PDF report, you choose where it goes.

Calendar

If you choose to import trips from your calendar, Staydar asks for calendar access and reads events in the dates you pick, on your iPhone, so you can select which ones to import. Events are not sent anywhere. You can turn access off at any time in Settings.

No accounts, location, tracking or ads

Staydar has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking code. It does not use your location, photos, contacts, camera or microphone, and it makes no network requests of its own.

Purchases

Staydar Pro is bought through Apple's App Store, as a yearly subscription or a one-time purchase. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app only receives a confirmation from Apple that the purchase is valid. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Crash reports

If you choose to share crash reports and usage data with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports. These do not identify you.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
