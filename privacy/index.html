Inchworm

Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Inchworm is a calculator. It works entirely on your iPhone and has no server behind it. Questions: admin@joshmartine.com.

What stays on your phone

  • The tape. Your last 500 results, with the expression, the answer, the time, and the name and summary of any Pro function result, are kept in a file inside the app. Clear the tape or delete a line and it is gone.
  • Settings. Fraction precision, calculation order, display options, haptics, keep-screen-awake and light or dark appearance.
  • Two usage counters. The number of results you have calculated and up to ten dates on which you calculated a result. They only decide when iOS may be asked to show its standard rating prompt (after 20 results on 3 different days), and the app records that it has asked. Nothing leaves the phone.

The memory register is held only while the app is running. Deleting Inchworm deletes everything above.

Inchworm Pro

Pro is a single in-app purchase handled by StoreKit and Apple. The app asks Apple whether this Apple Account owns Pro each time it starts; it does not keep its own record of the purchase and never sees your name or payment details. Apple's terms apply: apple.com/legal/privacy.

What it does not do

Inchworm asks for no permissions: no photos, camera, microphone, location, contacts or notifications. It has no account, no analytics or advertising code, and no network requests of its own. It does not track you. If you share diagnostics with developers in iOS Settings, Apple may pass us anonymous crash reports.

Children and changes

The app collects nothing from anyone, so it collects nothing from children. If this page changes, the new text will carry a new date at the top.
