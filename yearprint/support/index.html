Support

Answers to common questions. If yours isn't here, email admin@joshmartine.com.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Yearprint counts your photos on your iPhone and never connects to the internet. A card leaves your phone only when you share it.

Why are my numbers lower than I expected?

If you chose Limit Access when iOS asked, Yearprint can see only the photos you selected, and a banner says so. Tap Choose more on that banner, or go to Settings › Apps › Yearprint › Photos and choose Full Access. Hidden photos and photos in shared albums are not counted.

I said no to photo access. How do I fix it?

Open Settings › Apps › Yearprint › Photos and choose Full Access or Limited Access, then return to Yearprint. The app also has an Open Settings button.

What does each card show?

The year total, photos per month, your busiest day, your longest streak of days with at least one photo, the weekday and hour you shoot most, photos versus Live Photos, videos and screenshots with your favorites, the number of places (if Count places is on) and a grid of a few of your photos, favorites first.

How are places counted?

Turn on Count places on the first screen or behind the shield button. Yearprint groups photo locations into areas about 10 km across and shows how many there were. It never shows a map or an address.

Why is the highlights grid missing?

It needs at least four photos stored on the iPhone. Photos kept only in iCloud (with "Optimize iPhone Storage") are not downloaded, because Yearprint makes no network requests.

Can I make cards for last year?

Yes. Tap the year at the top to pick any year since your oldest photo. Until January 20, Yearprint opens on the year that just ended.

How do I post a card to my story?

Choose Story (9:16) or Square, pick an ink color, then tap Share card or Share all and pick the app you want, or Save Image to keep it in Photos.
