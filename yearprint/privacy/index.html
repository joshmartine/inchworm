Yearprint

Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Yearprint reads your photo library on your iPhone to make shareable year cards. It never uploads anything. Questions: admin@joshmartine.com.

What it reads

With the access you grant (all photos, or only the ones you pick), Yearprint looks at every photo and video from the chosen year and reads: the date taken, the kind (photo, Live Photo, screenshot or video) and whether it is a favorite. Photos stored only in iCloud are not downloaded; the app uses only what is already on the phone. These are turned into counts: per month, busiest day, longest streak, favorites.

The highlights card shows small copies of a few of your photos, loaded from the library each time. The computed numbers are not kept after you close the app.

Places

Off by default. If you switch on Count places, Yearprint also reads each photo's location and sorts it into a grid of roughly 10 km squares, then shows only how many squares were used. No addresses, no place names, no map, and not your current location.

What is kept between launches

  • Your card color and the Count places switch.
  • A share count, a whole number that goes up by one each time you complete sharing a card from the iOS share sheet. The app asks iOS for its rating prompt after your first and fifth completed share. It is stored in the app's preferences on the phone and is not sent anywhere.

Cards leave only when you send them

A card is an image rendered on the device. It goes where you send it via the share sheet. If you pick Save Image, iOS asks permission to add to your library.

Not present

Yearprint makes no network requests and has no account, analytics, ads or third-party code. It does not use your camera, microphone or contacts. It is a one-time purchase and Apple processes the payment (apple.com/legal/privacy). Anonymous crash reports, if you allow them in iOS Settings, contain no photos.

Children, changes

The app collects nothing from anyone. Any policy change will appear on this page with a new date.
