Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

Yearprint: Year in Photos ("Yearprint", "the app") does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. Everything the app does with your photos happens on your iPhone.

Photo library access

Yearprint asks for access to your photo library so it can make your year cards. iOS lets you give it access to all photos or only to photos you choose; the app works with either and counts only what you share. You can change this at any time in Settings › Apps › Yearprint › Photos.

For each photo and video from the year you pick, the app reads on the device:

  • the date and time it was taken,
  • whether it is a photo, Live Photo, screenshot or video,
  • whether you marked it as a favorite,
  • its location, only if you turn on Count places (off by default).

From these it computes totals such as photos per month, your busiest day and your longest streak. The highlights card shows small copies of a few of your own photos, read from your library on the device.

Locations

When Count places is on, Yearprint groups photo locations into areas about 10 km across and shows only how many areas there were. It does not look up addresses or place names, does not show a map and does not use your current location.

Nothing is uploaded

Yearprint makes no network requests. Your photos, their details and the computed statistics are never sent to us or to anyone else, and they are not stored after you close the app, apart from your settings (card color and the Count places switch). Cards leave your phone only when you share them yourself with the iOS share sheet, to the app or person you choose. Saving a card to your photo library uses the iOS "Save Image" option, which asks for permission to add photos.

No accounts, tracking or ads

Yearprint has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code. It does not use your contacts, camera or microphone.

Purchase

Yearprint is a paid app bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Crash reports

If you choose to share crash reports and usage data with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports. These do not identify you and contain no photos.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
