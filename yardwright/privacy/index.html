Yardwright

Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

Yardwright does not collect any data. It has no accounts, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your projects, shapes, measurements and calculator entries are stored on your iPhone.

Apple services

Satellite maps, address search and map images in shared reports come from Apple Maps. When you search an address or view the map, Apple's privacy policy applies. Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple; we receive no payment or personal details.

Location

If you tap the location button and allow it, Yardwright shows where you are on the map. Your location is not stored or sent anywhere by the app.

Sharing

Images, PDF reports and CSV files are created on your iPhone and go only where you send them with the share sheet.

Your choices

Deleting a project deletes it from your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.

Children

Yardwright is a measuring tool and is not directed to children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
