Yardwright

Measure the yard on the map. Know what to order.

Yardwright traces areas and lines on Apple's satellite map and turns them into material amounts for landscaping, fencing, paving and holiday lighting jobs.

Trace on the map: search the address, then tap the corners of a lawn, bed, driveway or roof. Area, perimeter and every side's length update as you go. Drag a point with a magnifier to place it exactly; points snap to corners you already traced; undo and redo every step.

Several shapes per property: name and color each one, and save the project.

Material calculators: mulch, gravel, topsoil and sand (depth, extra for compaction, bags, tons), concrete slabs (cubic yards and premix bags), sod, grass seed, sealcoat, paint and stain, fence posts, panels, rails and pickets with gates, holiday light strings and clips, and edging. You enter the numbers from your product or supplier; the few starting values shown are linked to their sources.

Share: an image of the traced map with the numbers, or a PDF report and CSV with Pro.

Free, with no time limit: unlimited measuring, image sharing, 1 saved project and 3 material results to try the calculators.

Yardwright Pro: $49.99 a year, $9.99 a month or $99.99 once for every calculator without limits, unlimited projects, the PDF report, CSV export and images without the Yardwright line. No trial that turns into a charge.

Satellite imagery can be months or years old, and trees and roof overhangs hide edges. Check on site before you order.
