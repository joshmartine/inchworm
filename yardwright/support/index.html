How do I measure?

Open a project, search the address and zoom in. Tap Area for a lawn, bed, driveway or roof, or Line for a fence, edge or roofline, then tap each corner on the map. Tap the first point or Done to finish. To place a point exactly, drag it: a magnifier shows the spot under your finger. You can also line up the crosshair and tap the ⊕ button. Tap a side's length to add a point in the middle of it.

How accurate is it?

Area and lengths are computed on the WGS 84 ellipsoid from the points you place, and the math agrees with GeographicLib to within 0.02% in our tests. The result is only as good as the imagery and your corners: satellite imagery can be months or years old, it can be slightly offset, and trees and roof overhangs hide edges. Zoom in, use the magnifier, and check on site before you order material.

Where do the calculator numbers come from?

From you. Coverage rates, bag sizes, densities, post spacing and string lengths differ by product and supplier, so Yardwright doesn't guess them. A few common values appear as short hints or one-tap presets. They come from QUIKRETE's data sheet for premix bag yields, the NRMCA's 4–10% concrete allowance, University of Missouri Extension for mulch depth, UGA Extension for tall fescue seeding rate, and Sod Solutions for common sod piece sizes. Unit conversions follow NIST Handbook 44. Your last entries are remembered for next time.

What does "Extra for compaction or settling" mean?

Loose stone, road base and soil pack down. If 5 inches of loose material packs to 4 inches, you need 25% more than the finished volume, so enter 25.

How does the fence calculator count posts?

Each side is split into sections no longer than your post spacing, and corners share a post. An open line gets one extra end post; a closed area doesn't. Gates are assumed to sit at a corner or the end of a run: their width is taken off the longest sides and each gate adds one post.

What do Free and Pro include?

Free: unlimited measuring, image sharing, 1 saved project and 3 material results. A result counts the first time a calculator shows amounts; results you already got stay visible. Pro ($49.99 a year, $9.99 a month or $99.99 once): every calculator without limits, unlimited projects, the PDF report, CSV export, and images without the "Measured with Yardwright" line. Cancel a subscription in Settings › your name › Subscriptions. To restore, open Yardwright › Settings › Restore Purchases.

Can I measure by walking with GPS?

No. Phone GPS is off by several feet, which is too much for a bed or a driveway. Yardwright measures on the imagery. The location button only shows where you are on the map.
