Tearly

A tear-off calendar for your iPhone.

Tearly is the page-a-day calendar that hangs by the kitchen door. Every morning there's a fresh page with the date, the moon and a line from an old almanac. Pull it down and it tears along the perforation, with the rasp of paper and a small tick under your thumb.

One line on the back: turn any page over and write a note, a mood or something you're grateful for. Every torn page is kept, so you can flip back through your year.

Share a page: send the front or back of any page as a paper-textured picture.

Paper and widgets: Classic red and black, Minimal or Vintage paper, plus Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets.

Support · Privacy Policy

What you write stays on your iPhone. No account, no ads, no connection needed.
