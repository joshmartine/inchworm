How do I tear off a page?

Put your finger near a bottom corner of the page and pull down. The perforation tears from that side. Stop halfway and the page stays half torn; pull further or flick it and it comes off. With VoiceOver, use the "Tear off page" action.

I tore off too many pages.

Tear-off calendars let you peek ahead, so Tearly does too. When the top page is in the future, tap Back to today under the pad. Those pages go back on the pad, and any line you wrote on them stays.

I didn't open the app for a few days.

The pad waits on the last page you didn't tear, like a real one. Tear them one by one, or tap Tear all under the pad.

How do I write on a page?

Tap Write. The page turns over and you can type one line, up to 120 characters. Tap Done to keep it. To change the line on an older page, open Pages, choose the page and tap Edit Line.

Where do the sayings come from?

Every saying is a short line from a public-domain work, such as Benjamin Franklin's Poor Richard's Almanack, Thoreau's Walden, Emerson's essays and Shakespeare. The source is printed under each one, and the full list is in Settings under "About the sayings". You can turn them off in Settings.

How is the moon phase worked out?

Tearly calculates it on your iPhone from the positions of the Sun and Moon for your time zone. New, first quarter, full and last quarter fall on the day the exact moment happens where you are.

Which week number does it show?

The week of the year follows your iPhone's calendar settings, the same as the Calendar app. In the United States, the week that contains January 1 is week 1.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold the Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit and then Add Widget, and search for Tearly. Touch and hold the widget and choose Edit Widget to pick a paper style.

Are my pages backed up?

Your pages are stored in the app on your iPhone and are included in your regular iPhone backup (iCloud or computer). There is no separate account or sync.
