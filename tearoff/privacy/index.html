Tearly

Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Tearly keeps a pad of daily pages. The lines you write on them are private to your iPhone. Write to admin@joshmartine.com with any question.

Your pages

For each day the app stores the line you wrote (at most 120 characters) and, if you tore the page off, the time you did it. They live in one file in the app's storage. If that file is ever unreadable, Tearly sets a copy aside in the same place instead of overwriting it. The file is not synced anywhere; like other app data, it can be part of an iPhone backup you make yourself.

Preferences kept

Paper style, whether sayings are shown, sound, haptics, the Tearly mark on shared images, the reminder switch and time, and a flag noting that the rating prompt was already requested. That prompt appears once, after you have written lines on at least three days.

Morning reminder

Only if you turn it on, Tearly asks iOS for notification permission and schedules one repeating local notification at the time you choose. Its text is fixed ("A fresh page") and does not include your lines. No server is involved. Switching it off cancels it.

The widget

The widget draws today's date, moon phase and, if you want it, a saying, all worked out on the device from the calendar. It does not read your pages.

Sharing a page

A shared page is rendered as an image on your iPhone and passed to the iOS share sheet. What happens next depends on the app you pick. Tearly does not ask for photo library access.

Everything else

Tearly has no network code, no account, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not track you. The one-time price is handled by the App Store (apple.com/legal/privacy). If you have shared crash data with developers in iOS Settings, Apple may send us anonymous reports. Nothing is collected from children or anyone else. Updates to this policy are dated here.
