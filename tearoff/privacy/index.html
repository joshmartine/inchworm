Tearly

Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

Tearly: Tear Off Calendar ("Tearly", "the app") does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

What the app stores

The lines you write on your pages, which pages you have torn off, and your settings are saved only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

No accounts, tracking or ads

Tearly has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking code. It makes no network requests and does not use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. The moon phase is calculated on your iPhone.

Sharing

When you share a page, Tearly creates an image on your iPhone and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes next is up to you and the app you choose.

Reminders

If you switch on the morning reminder, Tearly schedules a local notification on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to a server.

Purchase

Tearly is a paid app sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Crash reports

If you choose to share crash reports and usage data with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports. These do not identify you.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: see the Support page.
