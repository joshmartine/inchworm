Where do the starting prices come from?

From published industry pricing guides. Each rate in the price book shows a typical range. They are starting points: change them to what you charge. One item (fence staining per sq ft) has no starting price because no guide we opened gives one; it shows "Set your rate". A line with no price shows "No rate set" in the quote, and Send asks before it goes out.

How accurate are photo estimates?

They are rough. The estimator lists what it can see and sizes it from scale clues such as doors, garage doors and stories, and tags each line rough, fair or good with a reason. It cannot see the back of a house or under trees. Check every quantity, measure on the map when area drives the price, and keep "estimate subject to inspection" on your quotes.

How accurate is measuring on the map?

Area and length are computed from the points you tap. Satellite imagery can be months old, and trees and roof overhangs hide edges, so zoom in, tap carefully and treat the number as a starting point. Roof area uses the footprint times a pitch factor; wall area uses the outline times stories times 9 ft.

I'm offline or the estimator is busy

Your quote and photos are saved. Add items by hand or measure on the map, and tap Estimate again later.

What do Free and Pro include?

Free: unlimited quotes you type in, PDF and image export, 10 map measurements, 3 photo estimates and 5 written scope paragraphs per device. A photo estimate that adds no line items doesn't use up a free estimate (twice per device). Pro ($79.99 a year or $12.99 a month): unlimited map measuring, 150 photo estimates a month, written scope paragraphs, and your logo instead of the Curbquote footer. Cancel in Settings › your name › Subscriptions. To restore, open Curbquote › Settings › Restore Purchases.

Does Curbquote do invoices or scheduling?

No. It does one job: the quote.
