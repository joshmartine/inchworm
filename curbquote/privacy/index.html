Curbquote

Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

Curbquote has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your quotes, customers, price book, logo and photos are stored on your iPhone.

What leaves your iPhone, and when

Only when you tap Estimate from photos or Write the scope, after you agree on the consent screen:

  • The job photos you chose (resized and re-encoded, so camera location metadata is removed), your note to the estimator, the service name, the names and units of the items in your price list, and any quantities you already measured are sent to the Curbquote server.
  • For "Write the scope", the service name, the names and quantities of the quote's line items, the conditions the estimator noted and your note are sent.
  • The Curbquote server forwards them to Anthropic's Claude API to produce the estimate or paragraph, returns the result, and does not store or log the photos or text.

Your prices, totals, and your customers' names, addresses, phone numbers and email addresses are never sent. Anything you type into the note or into an item name is sent as written, so keep customer details out of them, and avoid photographing people and license plates.

Anthropic

Anthropic processes the request as our service provider. Anthropic states that it automatically deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days, keeps them longer only if they are flagged under its Usage Policy (up to 2 years) or the law requires it, and does not use API data to train its models by default. See Anthropic's Privacy Center: data retention and model training.

What our server keeps

  • A random key identifier created by Apple's App Attest when you first use photo estimates, with counts of how many estimates this install has used (today, this month, in total). It is used to verify that requests come from the real app and to apply the free and Pro limits.
  • If you subscribe, the App Store's original transaction number for your subscription, with this month's usage count, so the monthly allowance is shared across your devices.
  • Error counts and timings without any photo or text.

None of this is linked to your name or contact details, which we never receive.

Apple services

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple. Satellite maps are provided by Apple Maps; when you search an address or view the map, Apple's privacy policy applies. Measurements are computed on your iPhone.

Your choices

Turn off Settings › Send photos for estimates to stop all uploads; measuring and manual quotes keep working. Deleting the app deletes everything stored on your iPhone. To ask about the counts our server keeps, email admin@joshmartine.com.

Children

Curbquote is a business tool and is not directed to children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
