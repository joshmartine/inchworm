Curbquote

Bid the job before you leave the curb.

Curbquote is a quoting app for solo pressure washing operators and small crews. Snap the driveway and the siding or trace them on the satellite map, and send the homeowner a PDF quote priced from your own rates.

Your rates: set your price book once (per sq ft for concrete, siding and roofs, per ft for gutters, per deck or fence, minimum job, travel fee, sales tax). Starting prices come from public pricing guides and are labeled as starting points.

Measure on the map: trace a driveway, patio or roof on Apple's satellite map for square feet, or a gutter run or roofline for linear feet, with helpers for roof pitch and wall height.

Estimate from photos: take one to five photos and Anthropic's Claude AI lists the work and sizes it, each line tagged rough, fair or good with a short reason. It never sets a price. Your rates do, and you edit every line before sending. What is sent is in the Privacy Policy.

Send it: a PDF or image quote with your business name, line items, total, valid-until date and "estimate subject to inspection". Track quotes as draft, sent, won or lost.

Also quotes: window cleaning, gutters and leaf cleanup, holiday lights, snow removal, lawn mowing, beds and mulch, and fence and deck staining.

Free, with no time limit: unlimited quotes you type in, PDF and image quotes, plus 10 map measurements and 3 photo estimates to try.

Curbquote Pro: $12.99 a month or $79.99 a year for unlimited map measuring, 150 photo estimates a month, scope-of-work paragraphs written for you, and your logo in place of the Curbquote footer.
