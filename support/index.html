How do I type 5 feet 7 and 3/16 inches?

Press 5 Feet 7 Inch 3 / 16. If you stop after the /, Inchworm uses your precision setting as the bottom number (1/16 by default).

How do I change the fraction precision?

Tap the precision button (for example "1/16") at the top left of the calculator, or go to Settings › Precision. You can choose from 1/2 to 1/64. Results are rounded only for display; calculations keep the exact value.

Why does 2 + 3 × 4 give 14?

By default Inchworm does × and ÷ before + and −, like the iPhone Calculator. If you prefer a handheld construction calculator's left-to-right order, go to Settings › Calculation Order › Handheld.

How do I convert to metric or decimal feet?

After a result, press Conv and then a unit key: Feet, Inch, Yd, m, cm or mm. Press Conv Feet twice for decimal feet.

What does Inchworm Pro include?

Stairs, Right Triangle & Pitch, Rafter, Square Up, Area & Volume, Concrete, Board Feet and Studs. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. The calculator, conversions, memory and the tape are always free.

I bought Pro. How do I get it on a new iPhone?

Open the Functions tab or Settings and tap Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. Family Sharing is supported.

Which stair code limits does it use?

By default, the US residential code (IRC): riser at most 7-3/4", tread at least 10". You can switch to commercial limits or enter your own local limits under Limits › Custom. Always check your local building code.
