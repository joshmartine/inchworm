Clatter

A split-flap board in your pocket.

Type a line and each flap turns through its drum until it drops into place, with a clack and a pulse in your hand for every one.

Message: type anything, or tap Welcome Home, Happy Birthday or Now Boarding.

Clock: weekday, time and date that flip every minute. Full Screen keeps the screen awake on your desk or nightstand.

Countdown: days, hours and minutes to the trip, birthday or launch.

Share: a short looping video of the board flipping into your message, with sound, or a still picture.

Five boards: Midnight, Ivory, Amber, Seafoam and Tomato. Every clack is synthesized, flap by flap.
