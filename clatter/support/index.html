Support

Quick answers. If the board doesn't cover yours, email admin@joshmartine.com.

I can't hear the clatter.

Check that the button under the board says Sound On and turn up your iPhone's volume. If the ring switch is on silent, Clatter stays quiet too. It plays alongside your music when sound is on.

Which characters can the board show?

Letters A to Z, digits 0 to 9, a space, the punctuation . , : ; ! ? ' " - + = / & @ # $ % ( ) and a heart (type ♥ or the ❤️ emoji). Lowercase letters become capitals, accents are dropped (é becomes E) and other emoji are left out.

How much fits?

The message board has 6 rows of 14 flaps. Words wrap to the next row, and a new line in your message starts a new row. If a message is too long, Clatter warns you and shows as much as fits. The clock and countdown use a bigger 3 by 9 board, so a countdown title can have up to 9 characters.

Why do the letters spin before they land?

That's how a real split-flap board works: each flap can only turn forward through its drum of characters, so going from B to A means turning all the way around. Each module starts a moment after its neighbor and runs at a slightly different speed.

Can I use it as a desk or bedside clock?

Yes. Choose Clock, then Full Screen. The screen stays awake while Full Screen is open, so keep your iPhone on a charger. Turn it sideways for a bigger board. Tap once to show the Done button.

How do I share a video?

Tap Share, pick Video or Still and a shape (Square, Story or Wide), then tap Make Video or Make Still. The share sheet opens so you can send it or save it. The video is about 4 seconds: the board flips from blank into your message, with sound, and loops neatly. Turn off Small Clatter mark to leave the name off.

Is there a subscription or anything to unlock?

No. You pay once in the App Store and everything is included. There are no ads and no account.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Clatter never uses the network.
