Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

Pipewright Offset Calculator ("Pipewright", "the app") does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

What the app stores

Your calculations (the tape), memory and settings are saved only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

No accounts, tracking or ads

Pipewright has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking code. It makes no network requests of its own and does not use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Purchases

Pipewright Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app only receives a confirmation from Apple that the purchase is valid, so it can unlock Pro features. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Crash reports

If you choose to share crash reports and usage data with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports. These do not identify you.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: see the Support page.
