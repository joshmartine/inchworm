Pipewright

Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

This page covers Pipewright, the pipe offset and fitting calculator. In short: what you type stays on your iPhone. Contact: admin@joshmartine.com.

Saved on the device

  • A history of up to 500 entries, including offsets, cut lengths, take-outs and other function results with their summaries, in a file inside the app.
  • Your preferences: fraction precision, calculation order, display options, haptics, screen-awake and appearance.
  • A running total of results and the last ten dates you calculated a result, used only to choose a moment for iOS's standard rating prompt (20 results, 3 days). A flag records that the prompt was requested.

The calculator memory is not written to disk. The pipe, fitting and flange tables are bundled in the app, so nothing is downloaded when you look up a size. Deleting Pipewright removes all of it.

The Pro unlock

One in-app purchase unlocks the Pro functions. StoreKit checks with Apple at launch whether your Apple Account owns it. We receive no receipt, name or payment information, and the app keeps no purchase record. See apple.com/legal/privacy.

Nothing else is requested

No permissions are requested, and the app contains no accounts, analytics, ads or third-party code. The app declares no tracking and no collected data types in its Apple privacy manifest. Anonymous crash reports reach us only if you have opted in to share them with developers in iOS Settings.

Changes and children

No data is collected from any user, children included. A revised policy will replace this page with a new date.
