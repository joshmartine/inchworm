How do I type 1 foot 4 and 3/16 inches?

Press 1 Feet 4 Inch 3 / 16. If you stop after the /, Pipewright uses your precision setting as the bottom number (1/16 by default).

How is travel calculated?

Travel = offset ÷ sin(fitting angle), and run = offset ÷ tan(fitting angle). For a rolling offset, the true offset is √(offset² + roll²) first. Every function shows the formula with your numbers under "How it's calculated".

Where do the take-outs come from?

Long radius 45° and 90°, short radius 90° and 3D elbows use the ASME B16.9 center-to-end dimensions. Other angles use the geometric take-out, radius × tan(angle ÷ 2), and are labelled as an estimate because real fittings vary by manufacturer. You can always enter your own take-out.

Does cut length include the welder's gap?

Yes. Cut length = center to center − take-outs − gap at each joint. Set the gap your procedure uses (for example 1/16" or 1/8"); threaded ends add the thread make-up.

How accurate is the pipe and flange data?

Dimensions come from public engineering listings, and a row is only included when independent sources agree. They are not copied from the ASME or ASTM standards. Always check against your spec or shop standard. Flange stud lengths are not included because published sources disagree.

What does Pipewright Pro include?

Cut Length, Offset Cut, Elbow Take-out, Parallel Offsets, Miter, Saddle, Flange Bolt-up, Tank Volume and the full pipe data. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Simple and rolling offsets, grade, basic pipe data and the calculator are always free.

I bought Pro. How do I get it on a new iPhone?

Open the Functions tab or Settings and tap Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. Family Sharing is supported.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Everything, including Pro, works offline.
