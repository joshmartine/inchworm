Glowtube

Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Glowtube is a nixie-tube clock. The time comes from your iPhone's own clock, and nothing about your use is sent anywhere. Contact: admin@joshmartine.com.

Settings it remembers

Tube style and base, 12 or 24 hour time, seconds, separator blink, flicker, how often the cathode show runs, night mode and its hours, keep-screen-on choice, tick and relay sounds, timer chime, timer length, the wordmark and whether you have been told the timer needs the app open. If a timer is running, paused or finished, its end time or remaining time is saved too so it survives a relaunch. These live in the app's preferences.

Screen on while charging

To decide whether to keep the screen awake while charging, Glowtube reads the iPhone's battery state (charging or not). It does not store it or send it.

Timer alerts

The first time you start a timer, the app explains and asks for notification permission. If you agree, it schedules one local notification for the moment the timer ends and cancels it if you pause or reset. For that alert it writes its own chime, generated on the device, into the app's Library folder. If you say no, the timer just runs while the app is open.

Share pictures and videos

Glowtube renders the image or loop on the phone, saves it to a temporary file and opens the iOS share sheet. Save Image or Save Video asks iOS for permission to add to your photo library; Glowtube cannot read your photos. It counts completed shares only while it is running, to ask for a rating after the second; that count is not saved.

None of this

No account, analytics, advertising, location, contacts, camera, microphone or third-party code, and no network traffic. The app is sold for a one-time price through the App Store, which handles payment (apple.com/legal/privacy). Apple may send us anonymous crash reports only if you have turned on developer sharing in iOS Settings.

Children, changes

We gather nothing from any user. Revisions to this policy will be dated at the top of this page.
