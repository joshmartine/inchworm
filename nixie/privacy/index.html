Glowtube

Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

Glowtube: Nixie Tube Clock ("Glowtube", "the app") does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

What the app stores

Your settings (style, clock format, night hours, sounds and timer length) are saved only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Pictures and videos you share

When you tap Share, Glowtube makes the picture or video on your iPhone and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes next is your choice. If you choose Save Image or Save Video, iOS asks for permission to add it to your photo library. Glowtube never reads your photos.

No accounts, tracking or ads

Glowtube has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code. It makes no network requests and does not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone. All sounds are made on the device.

Purchase

Glowtube is a paid app sold by Apple on the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports. These do not identify you.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: see the Support page.
