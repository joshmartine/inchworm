Glowtube

A nixie tube clock for your desk and nightstand.

Glowtube turns your iPhone into a glowing tube clock. Every tube is drawn in code: the glass dome, the honeycomb mesh and all ten wire digits stacked inside, the lit one glowing through the ones in front of it. Lay your iPhone on its side while it charges and the tubes line up on a walnut or steel base.

Clock, timer and date: 12 or 24 hours, seconds if you like, a blinking colon, and a timer with presets from 1 to 60 minutes.

The Cathode Show: real tubes need every digit lit now and then to keep the cathodes clean. At the top of each hour Glowtube sweeps through them, tube by tube, then settles on the time.

For the nightstand: night mode dims the glow to a low red, on a schedule or with one tap, and the screen stays lit while your iPhone charges.

Glows and bases: neon, ember or argon on walnut, brushed steel or bare glass. Send a looping video or a picture of the time, or of any 4 to 6 digit number.
