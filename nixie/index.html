Glowtube

A nixie tube clock for your desk and nightstand.

Glowtube turns your iPhone into a warm, glowing tube clock. Every tube is drawn on the phone: the glass, the honeycomb mesh, and all ten wire digits stacked inside, with the lit one glowing through the ones in front of it. Turn your iPhone on its side while it charges and it becomes a row of tubes on a walnut or steel base.

Clock, timer and date: 12 or 24 hours, seconds if you like, a blinking colon, and a timer with presets from 1 to 60 minutes.

The hourly show: real tubes cycle every digit now and then to keep them healthy. Glowtube runs through them at the top of each hour, tube by tube, then settles on the time.

For the nightstand: night mode dims the glow to a low red, on a schedule or with one tap, and the screen can stay on only while charging.

Make it yours: neon, ember or argon glow on walnut, brushed steel or bare glass. Share a short looping video or picture of the time or any 4 to 6 digit number.
