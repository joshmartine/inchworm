Support

Answers to common questions. If yours isn't here, email admin@joshmartine.com.

How do I use it as a bedside or desk clock?

Turn your iPhone on its side, for example on a charging stand. The controls fade away after a few seconds; tap the tubes to bring them back. Glowtube keeps the screen on while it is open. To keep it on only while charging, open Settings and set Keep Screen On to While Charging.

The glow is too bright at night.

Tap Night for a low red glow, or open Settings and set Night Mode to Scheduled with your own hours (10 PM to 7 AM by default).

Why do the digits spin at the top of the hour?

Real nixie tubes run through every digit now and then so the unused ones stay clean. Glowtube does it as a short show at the top of each hour and settles back on the time. You can make it every 15 minutes, run it now, or turn it off in Settings. It never runs during night mode.

Can I switch to 24-hour time or show seconds?

Yes, in Settings. In 12-hour time the first tube stays dark before 10 o'clock, the way real tube clocks do.

How does the timer work?

Tap Timer, pick a preset from 1 to 60 minutes or use −1 and +1, then Start. When it reaches zero the tubes flash and a soft chime plays (turn it off with Timer Chime). Keep Glowtube open while the timer runs; it is not an alarm and cannot ring while the app is closed.

Is there sound?

Sound is off until you turn it on. Settings has a soft tick each second and a relay click when digits change. All sounds are made on your iPhone and follow the silent switch.

How do I share a video?

Tap Share, choose the time or type a 4 to 6 digit number (a year, a date, a score), pick Tall or Wide, and tap Share Video. Glowtube makes a short looping video of the tubes running their digit show, then opens the share sheet. You can turn off the small wordmark in Settings.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Glowtube never uses the network.
